# Desde Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0, dated 2026-09-01.

## Why Desde asks for this

Desde is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later, and that is not changing. Anyone
can already self-host it, modify it, and run it commercially under that
license. This agreement does not change what license Desde ships under.

What this agreement adds is a choice for the project later. AGPL alone does
not let the maintainer offer a different deal to someone who does not want
AGPL's terms, for example a company that wants to embed Desde without AGPL's
network-copyleft condition. A separate commercial license is one way to do
that, and relicensing the project to a different open-source license, if that
is ever needed, is another. Both need a clear license to every contribution
that is broader than what AGPL passes between contributors on its own. That
broader license is what you grant below.

You keep ownership of your own contribution. This agreement adds a license on
top of that; it does not take anything away from you.

This document is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Individual
Contributor License Agreement (ICLA) v2.2, the template that
`contributor-assistant` (the bot that will ask you to sign this) and its
predecessor `cla-assistant` point projects toward by default. The grants
below, copyright, patent, and the representations, are the same substance as
that template. What changed is the party you are contributing to: an
individual maintainer, Mo Chang, instead of a nonprofit foundation.

Questions about this agreement can go to **hello@desde.design**.

## How to sign

Open a pull request against the Desde repository. On your first pull
request, a bot comments asking you to sign. Reply to that comment with the
exact sentence it asks for: "I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign
the CLA." That records your signature against your GitHub account. You only
need to do this once; later pull requests will not ask again.
